⚡ Chapter 1: Electric Charges and Fields

Electric Charge

Electric charge is a physical property of matter responsible for electric interaction. There are two types of charge: positive and negative.

Coulomb's Law

F = (1 / 4πε₀) × (q₁q₂ / r²)

Electric Field

E = F / q₀
E = (1 / 4πε₀) × (q / r²)

Electric Flux

Φ = EA cosθ

Gauss's Law

Φ = qenclosed / ε₀
⭐ Important: Coulomb's law, electric field, electric dipole, electric flux and Gauss's law.

⚡ Chapter 2: Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance

Electric Potential

V = W / q

Potential Due to Point Charge

V = (1 / 4πε₀) × (q / r)

Capacitance

C = Q / V

Parallel Plate Capacitor

C = ε₀A / d

Energy Stored

U = 1/2 CV²
U = Q² / 2C
⭐ Important: Potential, equipotential surfaces, capacitance, combination of capacitors and energy stored.
