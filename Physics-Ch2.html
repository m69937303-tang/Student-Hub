Class 12 Physics — Quick Notes & Formulas

📌 1. Electric Potential

Electric potential at a point is the work done in bringing a unit positive test charge from infinity to that point without acceleration.

V = W / q

SI unit of electric potential is Volt (V).

📌 2. Potential Difference

Potential difference between two points is the work done per unit positive charge in moving the charge from one point to another.

VB − VA = W / q

📌 3. Potential Due to a Point Charge

V = (1 / 4πε₀) × (q / r)

Potential is a scalar quantity, so potentials due to several charges are added algebraically.

Vnet = V₁ + V₂ + V₃ + ...

📌 4. Potential Due to an Electric Dipole

For a dipole, the potential at a point depends on the charge, dipole separation and position of the point.

V = (1 / 4πε₀) × (p cosθ / r²)
On the equatorial line of an ideal electric dipole, the potential is zero.

📌 5. Relation Between Electric Field and Potential

E = −dV/dr

The negative sign shows that electric field is directed towards decreasing potential.

📌 6. Equipotential Surface

An equipotential surface is a surface on which the electric potential is the same at every point.

  • Work done in moving a charge on an equipotential surface is zero.
  • Electric field is perpendicular to an equipotential surface.
  • Two equipotential surfaces never intersect.

📌 7. Electrostatic Potential Energy

The potential energy of a charge q placed at potential V is:

U = qV

For two point charges:

U = (1 / 4πε₀) × (q₁q₂ / r)

📌 8. Electric Potential Energy of a System

For a system of multiple point charges, total potential energy is the algebraic sum of the potential energies of every pair of charges.

U = (1 / 4πε₀) Σ(qᵢqⱼ / rᵢⱼ)

📌 9. Capacitance

Capacitance is the ability of a conductor to store electric charge.

C = Q / V

SI unit of capacitance is Farad (F).

📌 10. Parallel Plate Capacitor

The capacitance of a parallel plate capacitor in vacuum or air is:

C = ε₀A / d

Where A is area of each plate and d is the separation between plates.

If a dielectric of relative permittivity K completely fills the space between the plates:
C = Kε₀A / d

📌 11. Combination of Capacitors

Parallel Combination

Ceq = C₁ + C₂ + C₃ + ...

Series Combination

1/Ceq = 1/C₁ + 1/C₂ + 1/C₃ + ...

📌 12. Energy Stored in a Capacitor

U = 1/2 CV²
U = 1/2 QV
U = Q² / 2C

📌 13. Energy Density

Energy stored per unit volume of the electric field is called energy density.

u = 1/2 ε₀E²

📝 Quick MCQs

1. SI unit of capacitance is:
A) Volt
B) Coulomb
C) Farad
D) Ohm
Answer: C) Farad
2. Electric potential is a:
A) Vector quantity
B) Scalar quantity
C) Tensor quantity
D) Dimensionless quantity
Answer: B) Scalar quantity
3. Energy stored in a capacitor is:
A) CV²
B) 1/2 CV²
C) 2CV²
D) C/V²
Answer: B) 1/2 CV²
