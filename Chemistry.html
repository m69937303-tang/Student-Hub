🧮 Important Chemistry Formulas

Molarity: M = moles of solute / volume of solution in litres
Molality: m = moles of solute / mass of solvent in kg
Mole Fraction: Xₐ = nₐ / (nₐ + nᵦ)
Nernst Equation: E = E° − (0.0591/n) log Q
First Order Rate Constant: k = (2.303/t) log(a/(a−x))
pH = −log[H⁺]
ΔG° = −RT ln K

📝 Quick MCQs

1. The SI unit of molarity is:
A) mol kg⁻¹
B) mol L⁻¹
C) kg mol⁻¹
D) L mol⁻¹

Answer: B) mol L⁻¹
2. The pH of a neutral solution at 25°C is:
A) 0
B) 5
C) 7
D) 14

Answer: C) 7
3. Which element is a transition element?
A) Na
B) Mg
C) Fe
D) Cl

Answer: C) Fe
