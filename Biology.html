📝 Quick MCQs

1. The functional unit of heredity is:
A) Cell
B) Tissue
C) Gene
D) Organ

Answer: C) Gene
2. DNA stands for:
A) Deoxyribonucleic Acid
B) Dinitrogen Acid
C) Deoxyribose Nitrogen Acid
D) Double Nucleic Acid

Answer: A) Deoxyribonucleic Acid
3. Which molecule carries genetic information?
A) DNA
B) Glucose
C) Lipid
D) Water

Answer: A) DNA
