🧮 Important Physics Formulas

Coulomb's Law: F = k q₁q₂ / r²
Electric Field: E = F / q
Electric Potential: V = W / q
Ohm's Law: V = IR
Power: P = VI
Magnetic Force: F = qvB sinθ
Lens Formula: 1/f = 1/v − 1/u
Photoelectric Equation: Kmax = hν − φ

📝 Quick MCQs

1. SI unit of electric charge is:
A) Volt
B) Coulomb
C) Ohm
D) Tesla

Answer: B) Coulomb
2. According to Ohm's law:
A) V = IR
B) V = I/R
C) I = VR
D) R = VI

Answer: A) V = IR
3. SI unit of magnetic field is:
A) Weber
B) Tesla
C) Henry
D) Coulomb

Answer: B) Tesla
